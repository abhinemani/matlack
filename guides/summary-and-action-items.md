# Summary and action items

A general meeting with no fixed script. Capture what was discussed and
decided, then every commitment anyone made. Treat all participants as
equals; there is no single interviewee.

## Summary
What was discussed and decided in this meeting?
- Cover each major topic in the order it came up, with the outcome or decision reached.
- Note disagreements or open questions that were left unresolved.
- Include specific numbers, dates, names, and documents mentioned.

## Action items
What did people commit to doing, by when?
- One item per commitment, naming who owns it and any deadline or next meeting mentioned.
- Include things people said they would send, check, draft, schedule, or follow up on.
- If no owner was stated, say so rather than guessing.
