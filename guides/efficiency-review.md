# Efficiency Review interview

A structured interview with a department head about where the department is
efficient today, where it is not, and what would help. Answers should be
organized under the question they address even when the conversation wandered.

## Organization Strengths
What would you say are your department's greatest strengths?
- What do you excel at?
- What do you think your employees are most proud of working for the department?
- How do you think the public sees your strengths?

## Service Delivery
Do you see opportunities for greater coordination and collaboration across departments?
- Are there areas across departments where efforts or services are duplicative?
- Are there areas where workflow could be more efficient and streamlined?
- Are there recommendations or goals cited from previous performance reports, not yet enacted, that you'd like to see implemented?

## Public Interface
Who do you see as your primary customers, clients, and/or constituents?
- How do you evaluate public satisfaction with services provided by your department?
- Are there specific areas where you think the public wants to see more efficiency?
- What would that look like (faster response time, follow-up, user-friendly interface, public information)?

## IT, AI, and Software
How are IT, AI and software being used to increase operational efficiency?
- What challenges are you facing using IT and AI to assist with operations and service delivery?
- Do staff have sufficient guidelines and training in using AI?
- What opportunities and challenges do you see AI offering in achieving efficiency?

## Administrative Processes
Are there administrative processes that get in the way of achieving efficiency?
- For example: state and city regulations, procurement process, bureaucratic rules, legislation, hiring process.
- How do you measure success of your programs and services (KPIs, goals, metrics)?

## Resource Allocation: Budget
What are the primary cost drivers for your department?
- Are there cost drivers you have no control over?
- How do you measure the true costs of delivering services in your department?
- Are there regulatory, administrative burdens or organizational factors that drive up the department's costs?

## Resource Allocation: Staffing
How would you describe your current staffing resources to efficiently meet the public's need for services?
- Are there staffing shortages, and if so, in what areas?
- Do you see opportunities to consolidate functions or share resources across departments?

## Priorities
If you had to choose up to three high-priority areas that you'd like the department to accelerate its level of efficiency, what would those be?

## Best Practices and Peers
Do you know of any best practices or innovations among city departments similar to yours that you'd like to explore or adopt?
- What professional associations, peer entities, or trade groups do you find helpful in learning about service delivery innovations?

## What the Review Should Achieve
What would you like to see this Efficiency Review achieve?
- How can it be most helpful to you and your team?
